AI-powered operations

03 — Systems I've Built

A full commerce stack, built with AI

Alongside my current role I run Global Fashion Professional, a UAE beauty and nail supply business. I designed and built its entire commerce stack myself — storefront, inventory system, Amazon operations dashboard, channel feeds and automated monitoring — using AI as the engineering partner. These are production systems I operate daily, not demos.

Customer storefront

Live
GFP Control Center — the custom admin workspace · figures redacted
Storefront on mobile

A Medusa v2 storefront and backend running the full purchase path: cart and checkout, card and cash on delivery, Tabby and Tamara instalment options, separate salon and wholesale price tiers, and stock states that stop promising same-day dispatch when a line hits zero. Checkout includes a map-pin delivery step so customers drop an exact location, which the admin order screen turns into a one-tap handoff to navigation or WhatsApp. I also built the GFP Control Center on top of it — a custom Medusa admin workspace that puts catalogue, inventory, orders, tiered pricing, B2B approvals and storefront content into one screen instead of scattered menus.
